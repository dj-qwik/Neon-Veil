# NEON VEIL: cinematic intro / trailer brief (generated-video edition)

**For:** an agent or artist with no prior context who will make a **high-resolution generated video** (not in-engine footage) capturing the essence of *NEON VEIL*.
**Length:** 75–90 s. It plays before the game's title screen (skippable) and doubles as the public trailer.
**Canon:** the story rules in §4 and §5 are fixed. Ask the project owner before adding new story facts, characters or dialogue.
**Reference:** `M00_contact_sheet.jpg` shows nine captures of the game as it looks today. Use it for **palette, architecture and mood**, not for exact shapes. The trailer should look like the *idealised* version of this world: richer, wetter, more cinematic.

---

## 1. Pitch

*A legendary operative walks into the most perfect building in the world as its deadliest weapon. Minutes later, the building decides they no longer exist.*

The trailer sells **the fall**: immaculate power at the summit of a rain-drowned megacity, the first quiet sign that something is wrong, then a plunge into the wet, neon dark below. Each cut moves us **down**, from white to black, from silence to pulse, and from control to being hunted.

## 2. The world: Veyra-9

- **Veyra-9** is a vertical megacity in permanent night rain, stacked in tiers. The higher you go, the richer and cleaner it is.
- **Crown Sector** is the summit. The **Directorate Citadel**, headquarters of an intelligence agency, stands there: a monolithic tower of white ceramic and black smoked glass. Through its glass the city is a silent ocean of amber windows, threaded with red and white ribbons of traffic far below. Nothing up here is dirty. Nothing is loud.
- **The Gutter** is the undercity beneath: wet concrete, rust, sagging cables, warm sodium-orange streetlamps, violet holographic adverts, steam, crowds under umbrellas, and giant propaganda screens showing the clean tower above.
- **AION** is the agency's artificial intelligence. It runs the building, its cameras and the city's security. It speaks calmly and courteously, never villainously, and that's what makes it frightening.
- **Propaganda:** "Directorate approval holds at 91 percent. Lower-district curfew extended indefinitely."

**Mood words:** immaculate, surveilled, hushed, expensive, cold, inevitable. Then wet, improvised, hunted.

## 3. Visual style bible (repeat these anchors in every shot prompt)

**Global style anchor** (append to every prompt):
> cinematic, photoreal, anamorphic 2.39:1, 35mm film grain, shallow depth of field, night, heavy rain, high-contrast, restrained colour, moody volumetric light, premium sci-fi thriller, no text, no logos

**Palette by act:**

| Act | Light | Materials |
|---|---|---|
| **1. The Crown** | Sterile pale white, matte black, a single **controlled cyan** accent. Perfect symmetry. | Polished white ceramic, white terrazzo floors mirror-reflective, dark smoked glass, carbon fibre, brushed titanium |
| **2. Betrayal** | Emergency **amber** and surgical **red**, hard black silhouettes, failing screens | Cracked glass, sparks, exposed conduits |
| **3. The Gutter** | Cold **teal** ambient, warm **sodium-orange** pools, **violet** neon adverts | Wet concrete, rust, oil puddles, corrugated metal, dense cables, steam |

**Signature motifs:** use these repeatedly.
- One thin **cyan light line**: down the centre of a black corridor floor, as a vertical light column, as the edge of the title.
- **Rain streaking down dark glass** with the city out of focus behind it. Rack focus is the signature camera move.
- **The lens / eye:** camera domes, a drone's glowing cyan lens, an iris that tightens. AION is always watching.
- **The 180 ms glitch:** a clean digital freeze or tear, as in one dropped frame. Never a messy full-screen distortion.

**Camera:**
- **Act 1:** slow, locked-off, centred and symmetrical, with long lenses. No handheld.
- **Act 2:** cuts tighten and the camera loosens.
- **Act 3:** handheld, rain on the lens, low angles looking *up* at the tower.

**Avoid:**
- Rainbow neon everywhere. Act 1 is mostly white, black and one cyan.
- Heavy chromatic aberration.
- Cartoonish or anime style.
- Recognisable franchise designs, logos or brand names.
- Readable fake text (keep screens to abstract data).
- Faces on the protagonist.

## 7. Delivery spec

- The video file is the deliverable, at **1920×1080 minimum (3840×2160 preferred)**, 24 fps.
- Use an H.264 or H.265 MP4 with stereo AAC audio, loudness about -16 LUFS integrated.
- Frame it 2.39:1, letterboxed inside 16:9.
- Also deliver:
  - a separate music-and-effects stem and VO stem, if possible

  - the list of licensed tracks used
