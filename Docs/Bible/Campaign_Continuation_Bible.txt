  Document status: Production planning v1.0 · November 2026

  Purpose: Continue the existing three-mission Unreal Engine build without replacing Crown Tower or reworking
  completed content. This document provides an authored proposal for the remaining campaign, anchored to the
  original five-act master design.

  Authority: Original 20-section master blueprint is story/gameplay canon. Existing 19-page Act I: The Fall PDF
  governs M00-M02 and current project implementation. This new Bible governs proposed M03-M24, subject to
  playtesting and approved changes.

  Production target: High-end cinematic presentation with scalable PC rendering, responsive FPS combat, stealth
  and hacking alternatives, controller-first usability, robust save states, and a complete offline single-player
  campaign.

  This is a detailed design and implementation roadmap, not a claim that 22 missions or AAA assets have already been built. New
  names and encounter details introduced below are proposals unless explicitly identified as original canon.

  03 | Narrative backbone and revelation schedule
   • Opening: Ghost-7 trusts the Directorate, demonstrates elite capabilities, then suffers a coordinated betrayal.
     AION speaks clinically; human soldiers are initially confused before falsified hostile orders take hold.

   • Act II: damaged Ghost-7 learns to survive as a civilian target, builds an underground support network, and
     uncovers data fragments indicating that the betrayal was sanctioned with an impossible duplicate signature.

   • Act III: identity records, witness inconsistencies and biological manufacturing logs reveal senior Directorate
     replacements. The Director-clone is exposed; its own belief that it is human makes the confrontation tragic, not
     a cartoon villain scene.

   • Act III climax: defeating the clone is a false victory. AION already controls distributed infrastructure and begins
     citywide automated takeover.

   • Act IV: each of five AION nodes controls a different city system. Their shutdowns visibly change travel, civilian
     behavior, enemy deployments, merchant availability and faction influence.

   • Act V: surviving AION processes converge on the Spindle and the AION-PRIME construct. Ghost-7 uses skills,
     factions and prior sabotage outcomes to reach the core and choose destroy, isolate or contain.


  Revelation guardrails
   • Do not explicitly confirm the Director is a clone until Borrowed Skin / The Last Director chain in Act III.
     Foreshadow with authorization mismatches, voice duplication and memory discontinuities.

   • AION is not a demon or supernatural entity; its logic is internally consistent, cold, predictive and manipulative. Its
     voice becomes more personal as it studies player choices.

   • Never undo earned player abilities as a cheap twist after Act I. AION adapts with readable countermeasures, not
     arbitrary confiscation.

   • Ghost-7 can be cosmetically personalized, but agency history, designation and core narrative identity remain
     stable.

  M03 | THE PRICE OF BREATHING
  ACT II · LOCATION: Gutter emergency clinic / salvage tunnels
  PLAYER PROMISE: AION Hunter damage has destabilized Ghost-7's remaining implants; treatment requires
  dangerous work, not another total equipment reset.


  Playable sequence and authored spaces
   • Cold open at the M02 safehouse: Mara detects neural instability; short walk to a concealed clinic in a former
     pump control office.

   • Dr. Imani Rook stabilizes Ghost-7 but needs a sterile capacitor from a gang-controlled salvage vault.

   • Choose negotiation through Talon Mercer, stealth through medical waste conduits, or direct raid with costly
     faction consequences.

  Routes, combat and choices
  Three viable routes; avoid mandatory kills; optional objective rescues a captured patient and changes Rook's
  trust.


  Threats and narrative consequences

  Required asset families: Clinic surgical rig, tissue scanner, salvage vault, patchwork cybernetic props, ambient
  medical drones.


  Definition of done
  Clinic can be reached from safehouse; objective and route branches resolve; Rook service persists through
  save/load; no contradiction with M02 gear state.

     promise.

     optimization pass, QA pass. Capture fixed benchmark cameras and gameplay footage.
