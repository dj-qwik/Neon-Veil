# M7 — M00 authored beats (Bible §4) — QA

Branch `milestone/m5-art`. UE 5.8.4, RTX 4050 laptop, play-in-editor.

## C00 tram arrival — `ANVTramArrival` (`Source/NeonVeil/World/NVTramArrival.*`)
Setup: `Scripts/Python/nv_m7_beats.py` (folder `Beats` in the outliner).
- The 12 `A_Tram*` parts (floor, roof, sides, rear, 6 windows, cabin light) are set Movable and driven by `C00_TramArrival`.
- On play start, if the player stands in the cabin, the tram is parked 60 m out on a new rail line. The rails have cyan guide strips and 7 support pylons.
- The tram waits for 15 steady frames (each step under 100 ms) so start-up loading stalls don't eat the ride. It then glides in over 14 s with an ease-out cubic.
- The player is carried by character-movement basing and has free look throughout. A front barrier blocks the open front until docking.
- Ride loop and dock sound: `SFX_Tram_RideLoop` and `SFX_Tram_Dock`, original audio generated by `RawAssets/Audio/generate_m00_audio.py`.

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| Log `C00 tram staged 6000 cm out` → `C00 arrival started` → `docked` | PASS |
| Ride wall time (diagnostic line on dock) | 14.05 s, 812 ticks — PASS (Bible: 12–18 s) |
| Player carried, camera free, can walk out after dock | PASS (visual) |
| Player not in cabin → `arrival skipped`, tram stays docked | Code path only, not exercised |

Fixed during testing:
- Summing `DeltaSeconds` stretched the ride to 19.4 s because the engine clamps frame steps during hitches. `UWorld::GetRealTimeSeconds` behaves the same way in play-in-editor. The ride now uses `FPlatformTime`, waits for steady frames before moving, and caps each step at 100 ms.
- Replaced deprecated `ACharacter::GetMovementBase` with `IsMovingOnGround`. The build is now warning-free.

## Operations gallery surveillance discontinuity — `ANVScreenGlitch`
- Trigger volume `D_SurveillanceDiscontinuity` at (11900, 0, 200).
- 1.4 s after the player first enters, all 8 `D_DataScreen*` monitors swap to `M_NV_ScreenGlitch` for 180 ms in two pulses. This is unlit, scrolling cyan-white noise. Glitch_13 plays.
- 1.1 s later the subtitles run:
  - Ops Monitor: "Gallery feed just dropped. One-eight-zero milliseconds, all cameras."
  - AION: "Correction. Feed continuity is nominal. No anomaly is recorded."
